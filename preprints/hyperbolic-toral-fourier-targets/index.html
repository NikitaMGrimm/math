Fourier decay for shrinking-target centers of hyperbolic toral automorphisms

Download PDF (24 pp) LaTeX source (.tex) GitHub folder

Abstract

For every hyperbolic automorphism A ∈ GL2(Z) of the two-torus and τ > 1, we study the centers D ⊆ P ⊆ V of balls of radius cλ−τn hit infinitely often from a dense set of parameters (D), at least one parameter (P), or possibly varying witnesses (V) on a prescribed arc. All three share local Hausdorff dimension 1 + 1/τ and Fourier dimension zero, yet optimal full-time Fourier decay splits three ways purely by arc geometry: o(1/log log |ξ|) on the unstable eigenline, o(1/log |ξ|) on every mixed line, and o((log |ξ|)−3/2) on every regular C2 arc with nonzero curvature somewhere in every interval (even at isolated eigentangencies).

A glued mixed-flat and curved embedded arc distinguishes centers with dense fibers (power 1) from those with nonempty or swept fibers (power 3/2). We also obtain sparse-time criteria, a totally real cubic Pisot d = 3 dichotomy, and a critical τ = 1 center whose fibers on every transverse affine line simultaneously exclude a prescribed decay rate.
