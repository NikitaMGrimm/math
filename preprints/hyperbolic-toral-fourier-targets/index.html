Fourier decay for shrinking-target centers of hyperbolic toral automorphisms

Download PDF (24 pp) LaTeX source (.tex) GitHub folder

Abstract

For every hyperbolic automorphism \(A \in \mathrm{GL}_2(\mathbb{Z})\) of the two-torus and \(\tau > 1\), we study the centers \(D \subseteq P \subseteq V\) of balls of radius \(c\lambda^{-\tau n}\) hit infinitely often from a dense set of parameters (\(D\)), at least one parameter (\(P\)), or possibly varying witnesses (\(V\)) on a prescribed arc. All three share local Hausdorff dimension \(1 + 1/\tau\) and Fourier dimension zero, yet optimal full-time Fourier decay splits three ways purely by arc geometry: \(o(1/\log\log|\xi|)\) on the unstable eigenline, \(o(1/\log|\xi|)\) on every mixed line, and \(o((\log|\xi|)^{-3/2})\) on every regular \(C^2\) arc with nonzero curvature somewhere in every interval (even at isolated eigentangencies).

A glued mixed-flat and curved embedded arc distinguishes centers with dense fibers (power \(1\)) from those with nonempty or swept fibers (power \(3/2\)). We also obtain sparse-time criteria, a totally real cubic Pisot \(d = 3\) dichotomy, and a critical \(\tau = 1\) center whose fibers on every transverse affine line simultaneously exclude a prescribed decay rate.

Three-Way Geometric Hierarchy

$$\dim_H D = \dim_H P = \dim_H V = 1 + \frac{1}{\tau}, \qquad \dim_F = 0, \qquad L = \log(e + |\xi|):$$ $$o\!\left(\frac{1}{\log L}\right)\ \text{(unstable)} \quad\longrightarrow\quad o\!\left(\frac{1}{L}\right)\ \text{(mixed line)} \quad\longrightarrow\quad o\!\left(L^{-3/2}\right)\ \text{(curved } C^2 \text{ arc)}.$$
