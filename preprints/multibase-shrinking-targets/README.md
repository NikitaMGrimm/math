# [Simultaneous shrinking targets on the diagonal in several integer bases](multibase-shrinking-targets.pdf)

**Pages:** 21 · **Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Files:** [PDF](multibase-shrinking-targets.pdf) · [TeX](multibase-shrinking-targets.tex) · **Web page:** https://nikitamgrimm.github.io/math/preprints/multibase-shrinking-targets/

## Abstract

We determine the local Hausdorff dimension of simultaneous shrinking targets for integer radix maps restricted to a scalar diagonal or an algebraic line. For multiplication by two and three, with common radius of lower exponential order $\tau$ in base three, each target has dimension $(1-\tau)/(1+\tau)$ when $0 \le \tau < 1$, and dimension zero thereafter. This removes the $abc$ hypothesis in a result of Li, Liao, Velani, and Zorin (*Adv. Math.*, 2023, §5.5, Remark 13). Explicit counterexamples refute both sides of their printed Eq. (75) at $(2,17)$ and $(2,6)$. For radii $3^{-n\tau}$, a single residual set of transcendental slopes gives binary–ternary zero-target dimension $\log_3(2)/(1+\tau)$ throughout $1-\log_3(2) < \tau \le 1$, refuting the all-bi-Lipschitz suspicion at $\tau = 1$; we compute the exact Hausdorff dimension of the slope sets at each exceptional fiber dimension. For finitely many radices at arbitrary relative speeds, a partition weighted by prime valuations gives the homogeneous dimension, and translated grid-interval intersections give the exact fixed-target law under shared primes (with $(2,6)$ realizing a full interval of dimensions as one target varies).
