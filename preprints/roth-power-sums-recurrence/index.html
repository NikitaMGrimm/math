Rational approximation to power-sum quotients and simultaneous recurrence

Download PDF (34 pp) LaTeX source (.tex) GitHub folder

Abstract

We prove Roth's exponent for quotients of positive integer-root power sums under the hypothesis of Corvaja and Zannier (J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux 2005, Remark (v), p. 740). Every fixed denominator range \(1 \le q \le Q^n\) with \(1 < Q < 2\) is admissible, and \(2\) is the sharp universal boundary. For vectors of \(m\) quotients we characterize the sharp range at exponent \(1 + 1/m\) by rational-function subspaces via arbitrary-rank Plücker weight descent.

As an application we determine the local Hausdorff dimension of simultaneous recurrence on the diagonal for any finite family of pairwise coprime bases, separate nonmonotone shrinking radii, and level-dependent translations. We also give a scalar cutoff for algebraic linear recurrences at a prescribed embedding (including repeated roots) and a cutoff for approximation by polynomials of bounded degree: for \((3/2)^n\) the sharp cutoff is \(3\) in every degree \(d \ge 1\), and for the trace ratio of the cubic unit \(X^3 - X - 1\), its real root \(\theta\) is the sharp height cutoff for quadratic approximation.

Main Formulas

$$\left|\frac{u(n)}{v(n)} - \frac{p}{q}\right| \ge \frac{e^{-\varepsilon n}}{q^2} \qquad (1 \le q \le Q^n,\; 1 < Q < 2,\; n \ge n_0).$$ $$\dim_H R_{b_1,\dots,b_k}(y; e^{-gn}) = \left[\min_{1 \le j \le k} \frac{\log b_j - (j-1)g}{\log b_j + g}\right]_+.$$
