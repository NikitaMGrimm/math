# [Rational approximation to power-sum quotients and simultaneous recurrence](roth-power-sums-recurrence.pdf)

**Pages:** 34 · **Date:** 2026-11-25 · **Files:** [PDF](roth-power-sums-recurrence.pdf) · [TeX](roth-power-sums-recurrence.tex) · **Web page:** https://nikitamgrimm.github.io/math/preprints/roth-power-sums-recurrence/

## Abstract

We prove Roth's exponent for quotients of positive integer-root power sums under the hypothesis of Corvaja and Zannier (*J. Théor. Nombres Bordeaux*, 2005, Remark (v), p. 740). Every fixed denominator range $1 \le q \le Q^n$ with $1 < Q < 2$ is admissible, and $2$ is the sharp universal boundary. For vectors of $m$ quotients we characterize the sharp range at exponent $1 + 1/m$ by rational-function subspaces via arbitrary-rank Plücker weight descent. As an application we determine the local Hausdorff dimension of simultaneous recurrence on the diagonal for any finite family of pairwise coprime bases, separate nonmonotone shrinking radii, and arbitrary level-dependent translations. We also give a scalar cutoff for algebraic linear recurrences (including repeated roots and complex embeddings) and a cutoff for approximation by polynomials of bounded degree (including sharp cutoff $3$ for $(3/2)^n$ in every degree $d \ge 1$ and sharp quadratic cutoff $\theta$ for the cubic Pisot $X^3 - X - 1$ trace ratio).
