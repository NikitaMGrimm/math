## Abstract

At radius $3^{-\tau n}$, $0 < \tau < 1$, simultaneous targets for $2^n, 3^n$ and $2^n - 1, 3^n - 1$ have Hausdorff dimension $(1-\tau)/(1+\tau)$ and Fourier dimension zero. We distinguish them by the rates at which the Fourier transforms of measures supported on them can tend to zero. For a regular decreasing gauge $h$, the sharp tests for the envelope $h(\log(e + |\xi|))$ are divergence of $\sum h(n)/n$ (critical $o(1/\log\log|\xi|)$) for powers and of $\sum h(n)$ (critical $o(1/\log|\xi|)$) for recurrence. The tests hold for arbitrary deterministic moving targets throughout the positive-dimensional range, including anisotropic windows for finite pairwise coprime families. Convergence forces zero limsup mass for every finite positive measure satisfying the big-$O$ bound; under divergence, each open interval contains a compact subset carrying a probability measure with little-$o$ decay and finite $s$-energy simultaneously for all $0 < s < H$. We also obtain absolute normality statements via PVZZ/DEL and $L^1$ phase averaging, construct measures on exceptional slope fibers, and treat shared-prime $(2,6)$ and $\tau = 1$ limits.
