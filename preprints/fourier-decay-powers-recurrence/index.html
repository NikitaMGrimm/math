Sharp Fourier decay for simultaneous power targets and recurrence

Download PDF (22 pp) LaTeX source (.tex) GitHub folder

Abstract

At radius \(3^{-\tau n}\), \(0 < \tau < 1\), simultaneous targets for \(2^n, 3^n\) and \(2^n-1, 3^n-1\) have Hausdorff dimension \((1-\tau)/(1+\tau)\) and Fourier dimension zero. We distinguish them by the rates at which the Fourier transforms of measures supported on them can tend to zero. For a regular decreasing gauge \(h\), the sharp tests for the envelope \(h(\log(e + |\xi|))\) are divergence of \(\sum_n h(n)/n\) (critical \(o(1/\log\log|\xi|)\)) for powers and of \(\sum_n h(n)\) (critical \(o(1/\log|\xi|)\)) for recurrence.

The tests hold for arbitrary moving targets throughout the positive-dimensional range, including anisotropic windows for finite pairwise coprime families. Convergence forces zero limsup mass for every finite positive measure satisfying the big-\(O\) bound; under divergence, each open interval contains a compact subset of the limsup carrying a probability with little-\(o\) decay and finite \(s\)-energy simultaneously for all subcritical \(s\). We also obtain absolute normality statements via PVZZ/DEL and \(L^1\) phase averaging, construct measures on exceptional slope fibers, and exhibit phase dependence at the zero-dimensional boundary.

Main Dichotomy

$$|\widehat{\mu}(\xi)| = o\!\bigl(h(\log(e+|\xi|))\bigr) \quad\Longleftrightarrow\quad \begin{cases} \displaystyle\sum_n \frac{h(n)}{n} = \infty & \text{for powers } (2^n, 3^n), \\[10pt] \displaystyle\sum_n h(n) = \infty & \text{for recurrence } (2^n-1, 3^n-1). \end{cases}$$
