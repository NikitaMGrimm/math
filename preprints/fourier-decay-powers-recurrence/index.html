Sharp Fourier decay for simultaneous power targets and recurrence

Download PDF (21 pp) LaTeX source (.tex) GitHub folder

Abstract

At radius 3−τn, 0 < τ < 1, simultaneous targets for 2n, 3n and 2n−1, 3n−1 have Hausdorff dimension (1−τ)/(1+τ) and Fourier dimension zero. We distinguish them by the rates at which the Fourier transforms of measures supported on them can tend to zero. For a regular decreasing gauge h, the sharp tests for the envelope h(log(e + |ξ|)) are divergence of ∑ h(n)/n (critical o(1/log log |ξ|)) for powers and of ∑ h(n) (critical o(1/log |ξ|)) for recurrence.

The tests hold for arbitrary moving targets throughout the positive-dimensional range, including anisotropic windows for finite pairwise coprime families. Convergence forces zero limsup mass for every finite positive measure satisfying the big-O bound; under divergence, each open interval contains a compact subset of the limsup carrying a probability with little-o decay and finite s-energy simultaneously for all subcritical s. We also obtain absolute normality statements via PVZZ/DEL and L1 phase averaging, construct measures on exceptional slope fibers, and exhibit phase dependence at the zero-dimensional boundary.
